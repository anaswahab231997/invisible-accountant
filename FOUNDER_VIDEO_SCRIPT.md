# 1-Minute Accelerator Video Script: Invisible Accountant

**Target Length:** 60 Seconds (~140 words)
**Pacing:** Confident, urgent, and conversational. 
**Visuals:** Just you, looking directly into the camera (smartphone is fine). No slides. Good lighting.

---

### The Script

"Hi, I'm Anas, a solo systems architect, and the founder of Invisible Accountant.

Five months ago, the HMRC MTD tax mandate went live for top earners. The final wave hits 3.2 million micro-businesses in April 2027 to file quarterly digital taxes under the MTD mandate. Right now, legacy incumbents like Xero are forcing plumbers and freelancers to become corporate bookkeepers. The churn is massive.

I built the antidote. Invisible Accountant is an autonomous tax engine that lives entirely inside WhatsApp. A user snaps a receipt at the trade counter, and our AI—custom fine-tuned on HMRC’s BIM37000 rules—categorizes it, cryptographically seals the digital link, and files it. Zero app downloads. Zero training.

The tech is fully built, backed by AES-256-GCM hardware security, and cleared for HMRC production APIs. 

I’m applying to [Techstars / Antler] to scale our B2B wholesale distribution through high-street accounting firms, capturing the £40 million day-one compliance spend. 

The software is ready. The mandate is live. The market is bleeding. I’m ready to scale this in London."

---

### Recording Checklist for the Founder:
- [ ] **Energy Check:** Speak 10% faster and 10% louder than your normal talking voice. It projects confidence.
- [ ] **Eye Contact:** Look directly into the camera lens, not at the screen or yourself.
- [ ] **One Take:** Don't edit or splice the video. Accelerators hate heavy editing. If you stumble slightly, just keep going. It proves authenticity.
- [ ] **Upload:** Upload to YouTube, set visibility to "Unlisted" (not Private), and paste the link into the application.
