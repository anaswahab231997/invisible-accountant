Cloud Infrastructure & Pilot Operation Notice

This platform operates under a pre-incorporation FinTech pilot initiative. Production application services run on secure ISO 27001 European cloud infrastructure (Frankfurt), with encrypted database storage situated in AWS London (eu-west-2, AES-256-GCM). Full consolidation of all container runtimes to AWS London is scheduled for general commercial launch.

1. How Our Service Works

Invisible Accountant is a digital record-keeping and workflow automation application designed to assist UK sole traders and their appointed accountancy practices with Making Tax Digital (MTD) record-keeping compliance (Notice 700/22). By messaging our dedicated WhatsApp interface with photos or PDFs of business receipts, our engine extracts financial data, identifies VAT breakdowns under HMRC BIM rules, and stages clean, editable Draft Bills directly into connected accounting software (such as Xero) complete with permanent SHA-256 receipt attachments. We do not transmit unverified records directly to HMRC without human review.

2. Our Responsibilities

We are responsible for maintaining the security, uptime, and strict data separation of your account. We commit to processing your data in accordance with our Privacy Policy and all applicable UK GDPR regulations. We stage your records accurately into your connected ledger as confirmed by you.

While our AI engine is designed to accurately extract data, you and your appointed accountant are ultimately responsible for the accuracy of your tax submissions and statutory tax liabilities under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007. You must review and approve all staged Draft Bills in Xero before authorising final tax submissions.

4. Accounting Platform Integration & Statutory Submissions

To enable automated staging, you or your appointed practice connect your accounting platform (e.g. Xero) via secure OAuth 2.0. Invisible Accountant stages records exclusively in 'Draft' status for human verification and audit. Statutory quarterly updates and tax returns remain the legal responsibility of the taxpayer and their accountant under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007 and Taxes Management Act 1970 s12B.

Note: Use of our software does not constitute formal HMRC endorsement. All statutory tax filings are finalized by the taxpayer or their licensed accountant.
