HMRC Audit & Sandbox Notice

This application is currently operating in a Sandbox / Pre-Production environment for testing and HMRC auditing purposes. While current testing infrastructure utilizes secure EEA-based cloud processing, upon receiving HMRC production credentials, our entire architecture will be migrated exclusively to a UK-based data center (e.g., AWS London) to permanently fulfill the UK-exclusive data processing clauses outlined below.

1. Introduction & How Our Product Works

Invisible Accountant ("we", "our") is a commercial software service that enables UK sole traders to manage their business expenses via WhatsApp. When you send a message or photo of a receipt, our AI extracts the vendor, amount, and category, and securely queues this data in your digital ledger for quarterly submission to HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) via their official Making Tax Digital (MTD) APIs.

2. Data We Collect & Legal Basis

To provide this service, we collect and process:

  • WhatsApp Phone Number: Used as your unique identifier and to communicate with you.
  • HMRC OAuth Tokens: Used strictly to authenticate and transmit your data to HMRC APIs on your behalf. These tokens are granted for an 18-month duration.
  • Financial Data: The amounts, categories, and vendors of the expenses you submit to us.
  • Fraud Prevention Headers & Telemetry: To comply strictly with HMRC legal requirements for the MTD APIs, we are mandated to collect and transmit 'Fraud Prevention Headers' during your transactions. This includes your IP address, device identifiers, and network telemetry data. This data is securely transmitted directly to HMRC for audit and fraud prevention purposes.

Our legal basis for processing this data under UK GDPR is the performance of our contract with you to deliver the Invisible Accountant service.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We implement rigorous security controls to protect your data. All personally identifiable information (PII) such as National Insurance Numbers (NINO) and Unique Taxpayer References (UTR) are actively masked and redacted before being saved to our databases. Your HMRC OAuth tokens and identity data are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM encryption, mathematically bound to your specific identity to ensure strict customer data separation.

We process and store all customer data exclusively on secure cloud servers located within the United Kingdom (London region).

4. Statutory Retention & Your Data Rights

Under UK law, businesses are required to keep financial and tax records for at least 5 years after the 31 January submission deadline of the relevant tax year (effectively 6 years). We retain your data to help you comply with this statutory obligation.

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, amend, export, or permanently delete your data. You can exercise your "Right to be Forgotten" or request a data export at any time by texting "DELETE MY DATA" or "EXPORT MY DATA" to the Invisible Accountant WhatsApp bot.

Important HMRC Warning: If you exercise your Right to Deletion, we will permanently erase your digital ledger from our systems. However, you remain legally responsible for maintaining your own statutory tax records elsewhere to comply with HMRC's 6-year retention rules.

5. Third Parties & Data Sharing

We use Twilio Inc. exclusively to route your WhatsApp messages to our servers. We transmit your finalized tax data directly to HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC). We never sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Security Incident Reporting

If we become aware of a personal data breach, we will notify the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) and HMRC within 72 hours, and we will inform affected users without undue delay. If you wish to report a security vulnerability, please email security@invisibleaccountant.co.uk.
