The Status Quo

The Behavioral Flaw of Tax Software.

Traditional accounting platforms rely on a fantasy of manual discipline. HMRC's Making Tax Digital (MTD) mandate forces millions of sole traders into complex portals they have neither the time nor the inclination to learn.

The structural failure isn't the tax code—it's the friction of the interface. Physical receipts rot in van dashboards, digital invoices drown in inboxes, and by tax season, billions in legitimate deductions are permanently lost to administrative drag.

The Antidote: Invisible Accountant
