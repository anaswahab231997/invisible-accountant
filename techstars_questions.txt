Team Members
1. Add each of your co-founders by providing their name and email. We will send them an invite to complete their own founder profile which will then be tied to this accelerator application. No worries if you are still building out your team - this step is not required to complete your application, and you can always come back to it later.
Program selection


1. In this step, you will be able to review Techstars accelerator programs and select up to 2 accelerators that you are interested in applying to. You will select a Primary preference and an Alternate preference.
   1. The program listed as the primary preference will review your application first, following the application close date. The alternate preference will be able to engage soon after.
