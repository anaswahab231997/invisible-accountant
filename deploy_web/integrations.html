Direct Ledger Integration

We extract vendor, date, net, VAT, and gross amounts, automatically checking for valid VAT registration numbers and applying Capital Allowances logic based on your chart of accounts.

Instead of publishing directly, it safely pushes the data as a Draft Bill into Xero, QuickBooks Online, or Sage. This ensures your clients never ruin the live bank feed, and you maintain total control over what gets reconciled.

Xero

Syncs as a Draft Bill with line items, tax rates, and original receipt attachment.

QuickBooks Online

Creates an unapproved Draft Bill holding state, ensuring zero ledger impact without your expert review.

Sage

Pushes structured invoice data into Sage Accounting for immediate approval.
